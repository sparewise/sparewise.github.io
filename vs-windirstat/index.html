Sparewise vs WinDirStat, WizTree and TreeSize

Short answer: WinDirStat, WizTree and TreeSize show what is big. They are excellent at it, and free or cheap. Sparewise tries to answer the next question: is this safe to remove, and will it come back? Many people will want both.

Use a disk space analyzer when

  • You want to see every folder and file on the drive by size. WizTree is the fastest at this; WinDirStat's treemap is a classic.
  • The space is your own files: videos, photos, old projects, game installs. You know what they are; you just need to find them.
  • You want to delete things yourself and do not need advice.

Sparewise helps more when

  • The space is in folders you did not create and do not recognise: .ollama, huggingface\hub, npm-cache, .gradle, ext4.vhdx. It says what each one is and whether it rebuilds by itself.
  • You want to know if something is safe to clear before you clear it. Only caches with a tested rule are offered, and everything goes to the Recycle Bin or an undo window.
  • Space is inside something an analyzer cannot see into: Docker's reclaimable data, old model revisions next to new ones, WSL disks that never shrink.
  • You want an AI assistant to help, within limits you set (Sparewise is also an MCP server).
