Why is my AppData folder so big?

Short answer: AppData holds every program's settings, data and caches. Caches (browser, app and developer tool caches) usually rebuild by themselves and are the parts people clear. Settings, saved games, mail and chat history also live there and must stay.

By hand, with Windows' own tools

  1. Open File Explorer and type %LocalAppData% in the address bar. Most of the size is usually here; %AppData% (Roaming) holds settings.
  2. Look for folders named Cache, Code Cache or GPUCache inside a program's folder. Close that program first.
  3. Clear a browser's cache from the browser's own settings (Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data), so your passwords and history stay.
  4. Do not delete whole program folders: they can hold settings, accounts, saved games or mail.

Questions

Can I delete the whole AppData folder?

No. Programs keep their settings and data there; deleting it can reset or break them.

Why does AppData fill up again?

Caches grow back as you use your programs. That is normal; they are clearing space only until the program needs it again.
