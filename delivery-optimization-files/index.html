What are Delivery Optimization files?

Short answer: Delivery Optimization keeps pieces of Windows and Store updates to share with other PCs. They are safe to clear; Windows downloads again what it needs.

By hand, with Windows' own tools

  1. Open Settings > System > Storage > Temporary files, tick Delivery Optimization Files, and choose Remove files.
  2. To share less: Settings > Windows Update > Advanced options > Delivery Optimization, and turn off downloads from other PCs.

With Sparewise

  • Shows how much space they use, as Windows leftovers that are safe to clear.
  • Points to the Windows setting that clears them.

Questions

Is it safe to delete Delivery Optimization files?

Yes. They are copies of update pieces; Windows downloads again whatever it still needs.
